# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 15+ App Router, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, explicitly requested by the client.

## Users

People in Charlotte, NC and surrounding areas planning birthdays, baby showers, baptisms, first birthdays, and other celebrations. They need to source event equipment, styling, or both without coordinating several vendors. The experience supports English, Spanish, and Portuguese speakers.

## Product Purpose

Present Pop Haus Party Rentals and Pop Haus Balloon Co. as one reliable event partner, make the complete offering and transparent starting prices easy to understand, and turn qualified visitors into rental or balloon-decor inquiries.

## Positioning

Pop Haus can supply and style the whole event: tents, tables, chairs, linens, concessions, games, and custom balloon decor through one cohesive local business.

## Operating Context

Visitors often arrive on mobile while actively planning an event. They compare inventory and prices, gather inspiration, then submit an event request whose availability, delivery fees, and final price are confirmed manually. Event dates are secured only after a deposit is received.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive marketing and inquiry website with a frontend-only request form prepared for a future backend integration.
- Rental and balloon pricing live in typed data files.
- Supports pickup and delivery requests, with a conditional delivery-address field.
- Video/reel components accept local MP4, external video URLs, or Instagram thumbnails; no fake embeds.
- Sticky navigation, mobile menu, accessible interactions, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.
- Primary offering balance is approximately 60% party rentals and 40% balloon decor.

## Brand Commitments

- Business names: Pop Haus Party Rentals and Pop Haus Balloon Co.
- Location: Charlotte, NC.
- Phone: (704) 916-9682.
- Balloon contact: pophausballoonco@gmail.com.
- Voice is warm, local, polished, and straightforward rather than childish or corporate.
- Visual identity uses warm cream and off-white, deep plum/burgundy, soft pink, and restrained champagne-gold accents, with elegant display typography and a clean sans-serif body.

## Evidence on Hand

The client supplied complete service descriptions, pricing, promotional terms, contact details, founder copy, and the desired information architecture. No logo, photography, social URLs, testimonials, live video URLs, or backend endpoint were provided; the site must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make the combined rental-and-decor offer unmistakable in the first viewport.
- Let specific inventory, pricing, and visual examples prove capability.
- Keep planning low-friction on mobile and make every next step obvious.
- Preserve real business facts and clearly identify replaceable media placeholders.
- Balance celebration with operational trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG-friendly contrast, semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus states, descriptive alternatives, responsive reflow without horizontal page overflow, and reduced-motion preferences.
